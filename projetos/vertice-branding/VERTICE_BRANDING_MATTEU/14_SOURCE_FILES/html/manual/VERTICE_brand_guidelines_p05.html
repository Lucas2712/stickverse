A gallery of choices, not a stockroom.
VÉRTICE is a premium sneaker boutique built on curation. Not a stockroom, but a gallery of choices: a short, deliberate selection of pairs presented with the care of a fashion house.
MissionTo select sneakers that deserve to be seen as objects of design, culture and identity.
VisionTo be the reference point for those who treat what they wear as a point of view.
AudienceDesign-literate people, 25 to 45, who see sneakers as part of a personal language: collectors, creatives and fashion-minded professionals who value authenticity, edition, material and story over volume.
CurationSelected with intention.
AuthenticityEvery pair inspected before it reaches the shelf.
CultureThe story behind the silhouette matters.
ExperienceFrom the first glance to the last fold of tissue.
