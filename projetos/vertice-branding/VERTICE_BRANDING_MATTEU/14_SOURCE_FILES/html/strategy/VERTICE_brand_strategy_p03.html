03
STUDIO MATTEU
Positioning
A gallery of choices, not a stockroom.
VÉRTICE is a premium sneaker boutique built on curation. Not a stockroom, but a gallery of choices: a short, deliberate selection of pairs presented with the care of a fashion house.
Mission
Why we exist
To select sneakers that deserve to be seen as objects of design, culture and identity.
Vision
Where we go
To be the reference point for those who treat what they wear as a point of view.
Audience
Who we serve
Design-literate people, 25 to 45, who see sneakers as part of a personal language: collectors, creatives and fashion-minded professionals who value authenticity, edition, material and story over volume.
