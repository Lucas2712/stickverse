The camera pushes in over the basket of grapes as it is tipped into an old wooden basket press; the grapes tumble down and the camera follows them into the press, where the oak slats squeeze them and thick dark purple-red must pours out in a glossy stream into a dark pool. Macro lens, very shallow depth of field. One continuous camera move, no cuts, no flash, no white frames, no zoom blur, no text, no letters, no logos.
